## The problem

The Fastbots system at DS Automotion was developed with the ambitious goal of
replacing traditional intralogistics methods such as conveyor technology. The
vision was that a swarm of robots would replace fixed conveyor systems for moving
items in warehouses, from the shelves to the packing area. The moment a swarm
comes into play, quite a few problems have to be solved. How do you coordinate
activities between the robots? How do you localize them? How do you avoid
collisions? How do you plan and replan motion efficiently?


## My role

I was the sole software engineer given the hardware platform, and I set to work
building the brain of the robots. This meant writing drivers for the motors,
battery, localization system and path planning system. Most of that was second
nature to me, given how many hardware modules I had worked with by then. What was
genuinely novel in these robots was the localization system and the planning
module.

## Approach

Instead of classical particle-filter-based localization, we decided to use a
novel image-based sensor that photographs the floor and localizes from slight
variations in the image. The sensor was an early-production product from
Accerion, a company based in the Netherlands. I integrated the Accerion sensor
into our robot, improving their SDK and fixing several bugs in their core
software in the process. That required working closely with the vendor and
testing their hardware and software on our robots.

Another challenge was to enable dynamic planning and replanning. The planning
module was developed by RISC Software and based on time-window scheduling for the
trajectories. Each robot was given a schedule to occupy a time window, and the
control software had to make sure those windows were not violated. I built
time-window tracking into the control software so that the controller could check
for violations and report them.

## Outcome

The platform kept going after I left. In January 2026 the FastBots Solution was
[nominated for the IFOY Award 2026](https://www.ssi-schaefer.com/de-at/newsroom/news/fastbots-solution-nominiert-1991536)
in the *Robot Warehouse System* category - a fleet of networked mobile transport
robots under centralised fleet control, which is the system those first three
iterations were converging towards. Today the system is a full-fledged product that has been tested in a range of
warehouses.
