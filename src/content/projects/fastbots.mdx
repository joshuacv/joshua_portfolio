## The problem

The fastbot system at DS Autotmotion was developed with the ambitious goal of
replacing traditional intralogistics methods such as conveyor technology. The vision
is that a swarm of robots would replace fixed conveyor systems for moving items in warehouses
from the shelves to the packing area. The moment a swarm comes into play - quite a few
problems have to be solved - how do you coordinate activities between the robots, how do you 
localize the robots? How would be avoid collisions? How do you plan and replan motion
efficiently?


## My role

I was the single software engineer given the hardware platform and I set to work building the brain of the robots. 
This meant writing drivers for the motors, battery, localization system and the path planning system. Most of these were
second nature to me given that I had worked with several hardware modules by then. What was quite novel in the robots was its
localization sytem and the planning module. 

## Approach

Instead of the classical particle filter based localization, we decided to use a novel image based sensor which 
would make images of the floor and then localize based on slight variations on the image. The sensor was a product in early stages of 
production from Accerion, a company based in Netherlands. I integrated the accerion sensor into our robot - 
improving the accerion SDK and fixing several bugs in their core-software in the process. This required closely working with the vendor company and testing
their hardware and software on our robots. 

Another challenge was to enable dynamic planning and replanning. The planning module was developed by RISC-software based on 
time-window scheduling for the trajectories. Each robot would be given a schedule to occupy a time window and the control software 
should make sure that the time windows are not violated. I developed the capability of time window tracking in the control software 
so that controller could check and report on any violations.

## Outcome

The platform kept going after I left. In January 2026 the FastBots Solution was
[nominated for the IFOY Award 2026](https://www.ssi-schaefer.com/de-at/newsroom/news/fastbots-solution-nominiert-1991536)
in the *Robot Warehouse System* category - a fleet of networked mobile transport
robots under centralised fleet control, which is the system those first three
iterations were converging towards. Today, the system is a full fledged product that has been tested in various warehouses.
