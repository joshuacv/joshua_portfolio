## 🚀 Featured Projects & Systems

Core localization and control software for a prominent, agile industrial mobile robot platform. Drove hardware integration, software development, and testing across its first three iterations, successfully taking it from prototype to a shipping commercial product.

### 🏎️ [F1TENTH Autonomous Racing](https://joshuacv.github.io/joshua_portfolio/work/f1tenth-racing)

Designed and built JKU's F1TENTH autonomous racing platform end-to-end (hardware and software). Replaced a baseline Nav2 stack with a custom 50 Hz Model Predictive Control (MPC) running on a Jetson AGX Xavier, securing 10th place at ICRA 2026 in Vienna.

Technical lead for EU-funded initiatives optimizing passenger experience and last-mile delivery. Focused on onboard occupancy detection and novel Ultra-Wideband (UWB) pedestrian localization systems to reduce collision risks in shared spaces.

### 🌊 [Underwater Robot Swarms (subCULTron)](https://joshuacv.github.io/joshua_portfolio/work/underwater-swarm)
> **Stack:** Swarm Intelligence, Distributed Algorithms, Embedded C/C++  
Six years of research on bio-inspired swarm robotics, formalizing a unifying paradigm for collective behavior during my PhD at TU Graz and validating it with physical robots deployed in the Venice lagoon.
